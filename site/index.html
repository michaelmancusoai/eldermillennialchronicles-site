The Elder Millennial Chronicles · The Tales Everyone Knows

Once upon a time, stories had teeth.

Nursery Rhymes So You Don't Raise Weak Kids

Somewhere along the way, the wolf got a stomach-ache instead of a supper, the woodsman learned to arrive exactly on time, and everybody got a ribbon for taking part.

Not here. These are forty of the oldest tales in the world, told the old way. Each one opens with its moral and stops one breath before the worst part, with three ink-and-watercolour plates to show you exactly how close it came.

Read them aloud. Do the voices. Let the silence do the rest.
