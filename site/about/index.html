About & Press

A family press, and a shelf of very old stories.

The Elder Millennial Chronicles is published by Mancuso Family Press, LLC, a small family publisher in Florida.

The authors

Michael A. Mancuso made The Elder Millennial Chronicles for his own children, Gigi and Enzo Mancuso, who are credited as co-authors. They review every tale before it is printed, decide which endings are scary enough, and veto the jokes that aren’t funny.

Michael also runs the series’ parent channel, Don’t Raise Weak Kids, about raising children on stories with consequences.

The series

The Tales Everyone Knows retells forty of the best-known fables and fairy tales the way they were first told. The series continues one region of the world at a time, forty tales per book, in the same format: the moral first, the story on the left page, three ink-and-watercolour plates on the right. See every book.

Every tale is also read aloud on YouTube as a “living book” episode with read-along captions.
